Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and login credentials, along with the expense records you create — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We also collect basic technical data like browser type and access times to keep the service running smoothly.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide and improve Spendly: displaying your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and securing your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which it will be permanently removed from our systems.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database services) solely to operate the application, and these providers only process data on our behalf.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
